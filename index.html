How to play

  1. Nations take turns. Move your units, then press End Turn. In a hotseat game, pass the device when asked.
  2. At the start of your turn each city yields 2 Supply and each capital yields 3. The count is shown beside the supply pip.
  3. Select a unit, then a highlighted hex to move. Forest, hills, swamp, and trenches cost more movement. Mountains block most units.
  4. Water stops an army, but a single hex of water can be crossed in one move. You cannot end on water. A wider sea needs a ship, and a ship on the crossing blocks it.
  5. Attack after moving, or instead of moving. Most units strike an adjacent hex. Field artillery and fortress guns reach two hexes. A unit at 0 HP is removed.
  6. Forest, hills, and trenches protect the defender. Trenchworks adds 1 defense to your infantry. Mountain infantry and Alpini may enter mountains.
  7. Fighters ignore terrain and cannot capture. Ships stay on water. A foot unit that ends its move on a city captures it, capital or otherwise.
  8. Select your city, if the hex is empty, and spend Supply to train. Locked units need research. Ships are launched onto adjacent water.
  9. Research shared methods, or your nation's own: Grand Fleet, Soixante-Quinze, Storm Tactics, Alpine Corps, Levy, Redoubt, Alpini Corps, Air Service, Irregulars, or Forts.
  10. You win by holding every capital, or by being the last nation with a city or a unit.
