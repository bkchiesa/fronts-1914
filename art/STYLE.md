# Meridian Fronts art direction (working title)

Look name: Khaki Plate. Original turn-based WW1 strategy art. Not Dojo. Not a copy of The Battle of Polytopia (no blob people, no candy hexes, no copied UI or names).

Palette: mud #6B5A45, khaki #C2B280, field grey #6E7268, olive #4E5B43, chalk #E7E1D1, dull brass #8A7044, ink #2C2A26.

Units: three-quarter tabletop miniature, 512 square, chalk background.
Terrain and settlements: top-down tiles, 512 square.
Nation marks: original circular badges in 1914 colors, not flag photocopies.
UI: one iron-and-brass frame, empty center.

Roster of five sprites per nation: infantry, cavalry, artillery, one unique, and a battleship or aircraft where that nation had the tech.
- Britain, Austria-Hungary, Russia, Ottoman: battleship
- France, Germany, Italy, United States, Belgium: plane
- Serbia: observation balloon (no navy)

Uniques: British rhomboid tank, French light tank, German stormtrooper, Austro-Hungarian mountain infantry, Russian armored car, Ottoman irregular rifleman, Italian Arditi, US Marine 1918, Serbian guerrilla, Belgian cyclist carabinier.

No text inside art. No core-guide or Dojo files.
